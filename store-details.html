Full description

Get more confident at speaking, one minute a day.

Every day Daily Blurt gives you one fresh speaking prompt: a hot take to defend, a story to tell, a question to explain simply. Think for a moment, tap the mic, and talk for up to a minute.

What you get

  • One new prompt every day, with a coach's hint if you want it
  • Honest feedback on your pace, pauses, filler words and staying on topic
  • One practical tip to try on your next take
  • A daily streak, levels and a history of your best takes
  • Challenge a friend with a short code and compare scores
  • Reminders you control, and a home screen widget

Private and simple

  • No account, no sign-up, no typing
  • Your answer is scored on your phone
  • Players under 13 see no ads and no analytics

Daily Blurt is free and supported by ads. There are no subscriptions and no in-app purchases.
